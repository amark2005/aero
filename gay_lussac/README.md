# Gay Lussac's Law
## I have used Gay-Lussac's Law for calculating the Pressure of a Tyre on track.

## Formula: `P2=(P1*T2)/T1`
**P1 = Pressure in the pit** <br>
**P2 = Pressure on Track** <br>
**T1 = Temperature in pit** <br>
**T2 = Temperature on Track**
---
## Tech:
i used **Python** to get and display values and **C++** for calculations
